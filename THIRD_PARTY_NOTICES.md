## Anime mascot portrait

The current application icon was generated with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool on 2026-09-05 and replaces the previous OpenGameArt portrait.

- Artwork: `assets/anime-mascot-source.png`.
- Prompt: Japanese anime chibi woman programmer with purple hair and a lavender
  hoodie, holding a laptop with a code glyph, three task status lights and a
  progress bar; bold contours and cel shading, readable at taskbar size;
  transparent background, no tile, border, backdrop, words or watermark.
- Processing: aspect-preserving resize with transparent padding and Windows
  icon sizes from 16 px through 256 px, generated by `scripts/build-icon.ps1`.
